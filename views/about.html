{{t:About BoltSpec}}

{{t:BoltSpec puts the tightening torques, service intervals and fluid capacities for BMW, MINI and Rolls-Royce in one fast page per car, made to be used in the garage.}}

{{t:Where the data comes from}}

{{t:The values come from BMW's workshop system, ISTA ([[SRC]]).}}

{{t:Check before you tighten}}

{{t:Values are for reference. Always check them against the official documentation for your exact car, especially for single-use bolts, angle tightening and safety parts such as brakes, steering and suspension. Work on these should be done by qualified people. BoltSpec gives no warranty and accepts no liability for how the values are used.}}

{{t:Translations}}

{{t:The Romanian version is a translation of the English ISTA texts. The values are the same in both languages; if a translation seems unclear, the English original is shown when you hover over or long-press the part name.}}
